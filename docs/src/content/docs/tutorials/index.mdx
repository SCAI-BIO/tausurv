title: Tutorials
description: Worked analyses of the PBC trial, from a first Kaplan-Meier curve to competing risks.
---

The three tutorials analyse the same cohort, the Mayo Clinic primary biliary
cholangitis (PBC) trial, and are meant to be read in order. Each runs top to
bottom and needs `tausurv[plot]`.

1. [Your first survival analysis](/tutorials/first-survival-analysis/): Kaplan-Meier curves, median and five-year survival, comparison by treatment arm and by histologic stage.
2. [Modeling risk with covariates](/tutorials/modeling-with-covariates/): a Cox model, hazard ratios, SHAP decomposition of one prediction, calibration and Brier score on held-out data.
3. [Competing risks](/tutorials/competing-risks/): death and transplant as competing causes, Aalen-Johansen against one minus Kaplan-Meier, and a Fine-Gray model.
